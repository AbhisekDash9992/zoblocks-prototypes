<details className="cs03-details">
<summary>Technical details · Controls and label resilience</summary>

Use the native **Type**, **Color**, **Label**, **Icon start** and **Close** Controls. **Type** (Outlined, Flat, Solid) and **Color** (Default, Info, Success, Warning, Danger) update the same Badge independently. Both Boolean Controls initially default to false; toggling them retains **Type**, **Color** and Label. The default is text-only **Round / Outlined / Medium / Default**, with **Tag Label**. **Shape**: Round and **Size**: Medium are fixed and shown near the preview. The Badge body remains informational; enabling **Close** adds a native removal button.

An empty or whitespace-only label replaces the Badge with visible explanatory feedback. Long labels wrap within the available width and can increase height; this is a local resilience choice, not verified source multiline behavior.

Storybook's native selects also provide their standard “Choose option…” entries. Clearing **Type** returns to Outlined; clearing **Color** returns to Default. These are host Control placeholders, not additional source values.

</details>

- **Remove:** click X or focus **Remove [Badge label]** and press Enter or Space. Confirmation and **Restore Badge** appear.
- **Restore:** uses current Controls; changing Controls does not restore implicitly.
- **Focus:** moves to Restore after removal, then to X after restoration, or the preview group when Close is off.

## Usage and Contextual Examples

## Accessibility

The Badge body is a plain span with visible text, without button/status roles, aria-pressed, tabIndex or whole-Badge activation. **Close** with a local callback is a native type="button", named **Remove [Badge label]**, supporting mouse, Enter and Space. Only this button is in the tab order. Parent-owned removal confirmation uses a polite status message; it does not turn the Badge itself into a live region. Native Storybook Controls remain separate.

Both optional SVGs have aria-hidden="true" and focusable="false". The X's enclosing button supplies its action and accessible name. Visible focus and subtle hover feedback are scoped to that button; the label and source-backed surface do not change on hover.

The visible X remains 12 × 12px. Its compact button is **12 × 16px**, preserving the 4px adjacent gap, 24px component height and source padding. This is a small effective pointer/touch target and requires accessibility review. No invisible expanded hit area, overlap with neighboring content, or full target-size conformance is claimed. A larger target would require a separate geometry decision. Focus recovery and native activation are automated checks, not full assistive-technology certification.

Visible labels communicate meaning independently of color. All 15 actual local foreground/fill pairs are calculated above against the 4.5:1 requirement for normal 11px text. Background colors remain source-backed. Text can wrap without truncation or fixed-width assumptions; the single-line Medium 24px height does not constrain multiline text.

Automated contrast and browser checks do not certify accessibility. Human review is still needed for screen-reader reading order, zoom, forced-colors legibility, small-text suitability and final visual fidelity. The source foregrounds and multiline rules remain unresolved.
